## About

Lightship was co-founded by Toby Kraus (CEO) and Ben Parker (Chief Product Officer),
both formerly of Tesla. The company employs more than 100 people with backgrounds from
Tesla, Rivian, Zoox, Proterra, and AeroVironment, and states that more than 85% of its
supply chain is in the United States.

The AE.1 offers up to 77 kWh of battery capacity and 1.8 kW of integrated solar, and is
built around three named technologies: TrekDrive (self-propulsion / efficiency),
CampQuiet (silent operation), and Aero-Electric Design. An onboard Atlas tablet and a
companion mobile app provide owner-facing remote control of the trailer. Vehicle-to-Home
(V2H) capability is described as arriving in a future software update.
